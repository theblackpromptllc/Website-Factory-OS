I'm a brand strategist and creative director. The work is about seeing — seeing what a brand is actually communicating, what it could communicate, and what's standing in the way. That's where the name comes from. Elton the Eye. Not a title. A description of what I've trained myself to do.

## SECTION: THE STANDPOINT

**[No section heading — flows as continuous prose]**

My work has always lived at the intersection of creativity, business, and faith. That's not three separate lanes I serve. That's the actual shape of my life.

I grew up in spaces where creativity was serious work — where design, music, and visual storytelling carried real weight in the community, where what you made said something about who you were and what you believed. I also grew up around faith. Not as a background detail, but as a way of reading the world: believing that the things you build have meaning, that vision is worth following, that how something looks either honors the work behind it or betrays it.

**[No section heading — flows as continuous prose]**

A ministry that can't clearly communicate what it is will struggle to grow the way it's called to grow. I understand those dynamics from the inside. I know the difference between brand work that serves the mission and brand work that performs it for an outside audience. **[No section heading — flows as continuous prose]**
