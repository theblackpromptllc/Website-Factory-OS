1. STRATEGY SUMMARY
================================================================

Positioning (one paragraph):
Rooted Hair Studio is the single trusted studio in Durham for the whole natural-hair
journey — locs, silk press, protective styles, and scalp/root health — done at a
specialist's level, not a generalist's. It is built for Black women who refuse to choose
between a loctician, a braider, a silk-press salon, and someone who actually protects their
scalp and edges, and who are tired of trusting their type 3–4 hair and locs to people who
treat it as a surcharge or an afterthought. Where the established Durham players win on
decades of word-of-mouth, Rooted wins on a clear point of view (healthy scalp + healthy
roots), explicit heat-damage-safe and low-tension promises, and a modern, culturally
authentic website with real textured-hair photos, transparent pricing, and instant booking.

Primary audience: Persona D — "The New-to-Durham Searcher" leads the SITE (not necessarily
the business). See §2 for why the site prioritizes her while the chair serves all four.

The one thing this site must do: Convert a Black woman who does not yet have a trusted
Durham stylist into a booked appointment, by proving — through her vocabulary, real photos
of hair like hers, reviews, and an explicit specialization promise — that Rooted genuinely
gets her texture/locs, then making booking instant.

2. PRIMARY AUDIENCE AND PRIORITY
================================================================

FACT (Research §4): Four personas were identified — A "The Loc Journey Keeper," B "The
Silk-Press, Heat-Damage-Wary Professional," C "The Protective-Style Planner," D "The
New-to-Durham Searcher."
FACT (Research §4, Persona D): RTP/universities continuously bring in Black women with no
local stylist network; she relies on reviews, real photos of textured hair like hers, and
easy online booking; a generic salon site with only straight-hair photos signals "not for
us" and she bounces. The research names Persona D as "the single biggest argument for a
strong web presence."
FACT (Research §6, Gap 6): Established competitors rely on decades of word-of-mouth and have
weaker web capture; the New-to-Durham/RTP transplant is "Rooted's clearest competitive
wedge for this continuously-renewing audience."

DECISION — The website leads with Persona D (New-to-Durham Searcher).
Justification: The site's job is the job a website can win that word-of-mouth cannot. Tenured
competitors already own the in-network referral; the research says they are weakest exactly
where Persona D decides — online, fast, on reviews + real photos + booking (Research §6 Gap
6). Leading the site with her is where Rooted's web investment has the highest marginal
return. This does not mean she is the most valuable chair-revenue client; it means she is
the client the SITE is the deciding factor for.

DECISION — Persona A (Loc Journey Keeper) is the primary RETENTION/revenue priority and the
co-lead of the homepage.
Justification: FACT (Research §3 #2, §4 Persona A): loc retwist is recurring revenue, clients
return every 4–8 weeks; established loc shops "own that repeat relationship." Recurring loc
maintenance is the most durable revenue line, so the site must speak to her early and
credibly (scalp/root health, no over-tightening, reliable scheduling) — but she converts on
proof of skill and consistency, which the site supports rather than wins outright.

DECISION — Personas B (Heat-Damage-Wary) and C (Protective-Style Planner) are secondary on
the site and served by dedicated service pages, not the homepage lead.
Justification: FACT (Research §6 Gap 3, Gap 4): heat-damage-safe silk press stated explicitly
is a "low-cost, high-trust differentiator," and braids/protective clients want transparent
price/time up front for a $150–$330, multi-hour commitment (Research §2, §4 Persona C). These
are high-intent conversion targets but are reached through service + city long-tail search
(§5) landing on service pages, not through the homepage hero.

DEFAULT-CHECK: The site does NOT default to a general beauty consumer. Across all four
personas the research is explicit that the deciding factor is trust that the stylist
specializes in HER texture/locs — not price or proximity alone (Research §4 default-check).
Every priority above is set on that axis. No persona here is a white/mainstream default; the
secondary ranking of B and C is about which channel (homepage vs. service page) reaches them,
not about whose hair counts.

3. POSITIONING STATEMENT
================================================================

For Black women in Durham who want their full natural-hair journey — locs, silk press,
protective styles, and real scalp/root care — handled in one place by someone who truly
specializes in their texture, Rooted Hair Studio is the natural-hair studio that does all of
it at a specialist's level and leads with healthy scalp and healthy roots, not just finished
styles.

Why it is different from the competitors named in the research:
- vs. Natural Roots by Jey (FACT, §3 #1: locs-only, no chemicals/no extensions): Rooted does
  NOT impose a purist boundary — it serves the client who wants locs AND the occasional silk
  press AND protective styles without being turned away. DECISION grounded in §3 #1 analysis
  ("a salon offering both… can capture clients Jey deliberately turns away") and §6 Gap 1.
- vs. Amaka's (FACT, §3 #6: upscale multi-service spa — hair, barbering, lash): Rooted is a
  focused natural-hair studio, not a broad spa. DECISION grounded in §3 #6 analysis: "spa
  breadth can dilute the 'she truly specializes in MY hair' feeling that a focused
  natural-hair studio can own."
- vs. Taji (FACT, §3 #7: 20+ yrs, full natural-hair menu, ~4.4/100 reviews): Rooted competes
  on consistency and experience, the "slightly-below-5.0… consistency/experience friction a
  newer studio could win on" (§3 #7), plus modern booking and an explicit scalp/root-health
  point of view Taji does not lead with.
- vs. Stylz Unlimited (FACT, §3 #10: cuts, color, relaxing, natural, silkouts): Rooted signals
  unambiguous specialization in textured/natural hair and locs only. DECISION grounded in §6
  Gap 5 and §3 #10 default-check: "we specialize in YOUR hair, only" beats "we do everything
  including relaxers" for a heat-damage-wary or loc-committed client.
- vs. CanaryCoiffure (FACT, §3 #3: 5.0/54 reviews, locs+braids, "affordable + skilled +
  clean"): This is the strongest hybrid benchmark, not a position to out-promise on price.
  DECISION: Rooted differentiates on scalp/root-health specialization and web/booking
  experience (§6 Gap 2, Gap 4), NOT on undercutting CanaryCoiffure's affordability claim,
  which the research flags as already strong.

The single position Rooted claims: "One trusted studio for your whole natural-hair journey —
led by healthy scalp and healthy roots." (DECISION, grounded in §6 Gap 1 + Gap 2, the two
gaps the research rates as differentiated AND on-brand to the name "Rooted.")

5. BRAND VOICE AND TONE (lens applied)
================================================================

How it should sound (DECISION, grounded in §4 cultural reads + §5 culturally specific search):
- Knowledgeable insider, not explainer. She is not a beginner being taught what a silk press
  is; the voice assumes shared fluency. Warm, direct, confident — a trusted specialist, not a
  spa selling pampering.
- Health-first and honest. Because the position is scalp/root health (§6 Gap 2) and the core
  fears are damage (§4 B, §6 Gap 3) and over-tightening (§4 A), the voice is straight about
  what protects her hair — not hype.
- Respectful of meaning. Locs carry cultural and personal meaning; the voice treats the loc
  journey with respect, never as "dreads" or as a novelty (FACT: §4 Persona A cultural read).

Language to USE (FACT, Research §5 culturally specific search behavior — use her vocabulary
exactly): "silk press," "retwist," "starter locs," "knotless," "protective styles," "4c
hair," "type 3–4 / textured hair," "loc journey," "scalp health," "edges," "low-tension,"
"heat-damage-safe." Using her exact terms is also the SEO requirement (§5) — the copy and
metadata must match her queries.

Language that signals "NOT for us" — avoid (FACT, Research §5 + §3 #10 default-check):
- Generic mainstream-salon terms: "blowout," "hair straightening" (use "silk press"),
  "relaxer/silkout" as a selling point (signals generalist; §3 #10).
- "Dreads" (use "locs").
- "We do everything / all hair types welcome" generalist framing (§6 Gap 5 — it reads as
  less specialized to a heat-damage-wary or loc-committed client).
- Treating textured hair as a "specialty" surcharge or add-on (§1 default-check — textured
  hair IS the center here, not the exception).

DEFAULT-CHECK on voice: The voice is written FROM her standpoint as the default reader. It
does not pause to explain Black hair to an outside/white reader, and it does not soften the
specialization into a neutral "everyone welcome" value prop — doing so would recenter the
default the research explicitly warns against (§1, §6 Gap 5).

6. CONVERSION STRATEGY
================================================================

Primary action (DECISION): Book an appointment online (instant booking).
Grounded in: §4 Persona D (decides fast on easy online booking) and §6 Gap 4 (aggregators
win partly because salon sites under-deliver on booking) and §6 Gap 6 (web/booking capture is
the competitive wedge).

The journey from landing to that action:
1. Land (often from "[service] Durham" search or brand search; §5). Within seconds, prove
   "this is for HER hair": real textured-hair/loc photos + her vocabulary + visible
   specialization. FACT: §4 Persona D ("generic salon site… she bounces"), §5 visual proof.
2. Reassure: surface the relevant fear-answer fast — heat-damage-safe (B), low-tension/scalp
   health (A, C). FACT: §6 Gap 2, Gap 3.
3. Show proof: reviews and a real portfolio of hair like hers. FACT: §5 (reviews are
   effectively a conversion factor; visual proof is part of selection).
4. Make cost/time clear: transparent pricing tiers + realistic time estimates, especially for
   protective styles. FACT: §6 Gap 4, §4 Persona C.
5. Convert: instant online booking, low-friction, mobile-first. FACT: §5 ("near me" + mobile
   intent dominant), §6 Gap 4.

Friction to remove (each from a research FACT):
- No real textured-hair photos / only generic imagery → bounce. (§4 Persona D, §5.)
- Hidden or absent pricing/time for a $150–$330 multi-hour commitment. (§4 Persona C, §6 Gap 4.)
- Forcing a phone call or manual request instead of instant booking. (§6 Gap 4; aggregators
  win on this.)
- Not mobile-first / weak "near me" map presence. (§5 #3 — Google Business Profile + consistent
  NAP "as important as the website itself.")

Trust signals to LEAD with (DECISION, grounded in §4 + §5 + §6):
1. Real portfolio photos of type 3–4 hair and locs (the #1 visual proof; §5, §4 Persona D).
2. Explicit specialization statement — textured/natural hair and locs only (§6 Gap 5).
3. Scalp/root-health + heat-damage-safe + low-tension promises (§6 Gap 2, Gap 3; §4 A/B/C).
4. Reviews / word-of-mouth made easy to read and share (§5 — reviews are a ranking AND
   conversion factor; Black-Durham community + IG referrals feed branded search).

Channel note (FACT, §5): Aggregators (Booksy/StyleSeat/Vagaro/GlossGenius) outrank salon
sites and discovery also runs through #BlackLocticians and Black-stylist apps. DECISION:
The site strategy is "own brand-name search + the reassurance/education content aggregators
don't provide, while MAINTAINING the aggregator/Google Business listings" (§5 analysis #4).
The website complements, not replaces, the aggregator presence.
